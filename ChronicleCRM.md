# ChronicleCRM

**ChronicleCRM** is an AI-native, modular relationship intelligence platform designed for **traceable decisions, human accountability, and natural language-driven prospect record keeping**.

> Built for traceable decisions, not hidden automation.

ChronicleCRM transforms every interaction, lead, and workflow into a living, auditable record of human and AI collaboration.

---

## Key Principles

- Every lead has a history
- Every decision has an owner
- Every AI action is explainable
- Nothing is silently automated
- All records are traceable and auditable
