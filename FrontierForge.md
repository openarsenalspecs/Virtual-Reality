# FrontierForge

### Ideas Into Reality.

FrontierForge is an AGPL 3.0+ open-source research, invention, and engineering platform that combines artificial intelligence, scientific research, semiconductor analysis, simulation, knowledge discovery, and autonomous innovation workflows into a unified system. The platform is designed to help researchers, engineers, inventors, organizations, and open-source communities transform ideas into real-world innovations.

FrontierForge functions as a research operating system capable of discovering opportunities, generating new concepts, analyzing scientific literature, exploring semiconductor architectures, validating designs through simulation, and producing technical documentation.
