# SymbolAtlas

## An open architecture for the human imagination.

SymbolAtlas is an open specification for personalized symbolic intelligence, designed to help individuals explore active imagination, understand their unique dream language, discover heartfelt solutions, and connect inner experiences with meaningful knowledge, places, and experiences in the external world.

SymbolAtlas is built on the principle that every person has their own symbolic language. Symbols, imagery, emotions, memories, and personal associations cannot be universally interpreted. The AI assistant acts as a guide that helps users discover their own meanings rather than assigning fixed definitions.

The system provides a personalized AI tutor for learning active imagination as a "dream awake" practice: a conscious exploration of inner imagery to uncover insights, perspectives, and solutions aligned with the user's values, emotions, and personal understanding.

---

# Specification Goals

SymbolAtlas provides an open architecture for:

- Personalized active imagination education
- Individual symbolic language discovery
- AI-guided reflection and problem solving
- Symbolic imagery exploration
- Personal knowledge mapping
- Geographic and cultural discovery
- Meaningful destination discovery
- Human-centered AI guidance

The specification ensures that AI remains a facilitator of exploration rather than an authority over personal meaning.

---

# Modular Design

SymbolAtlas follows a modular architecture where each capability functions as an independent component while communicating through shared services and event-driven systems.

The core system provides essential symbolic intelligence, personalization, retrieval, and discovery capabilities.

Optional plugin modules extend the platform with additional learning, creativity, community, and exploration features.

---

# Core Modules

## Personal Symbol Language Engine

The foundation of SymbolAtlas.

The Personal Symbol Language Engine creates an individualized understanding model based on each user's experiences, emotions, memories, and associations.

Features:

- Personal symbol dictionary creation
- User-defined symbol meanings
- Emotional association tracking
- Recurring imagery identification
- Symbol relationship mapping
- Personal archetype discovery
- Symbol evolution over time

Core principle:

> The user's experience defines the meaning of their symbols.

The AI asks reflective questions rather than providing universal interpretations.
